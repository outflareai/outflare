Slack connector: connect your own Slack and post each kind of alert, such as hot replies, to a channel you pick under Settings → Alerts.

Upgrade: to offer one-click Slack sign-in, set `CONNECTOR_SLACK_CLIENT_ID` and `CONNECTOR_SLACK_CLIENT_SECRET` from a Slack app of your own. Without them, people connect with their Slack app's bot token.
